Privacy & Credits

Updated 24 November 2026 · v0.1.0 testing build

MAZ Neon Rush stores coins, selected cars and best distance locally on your device. This build has no account, analytics, backend, paid purchases or active advertising. Clearing app data removes your progress.

The source includes an optional AdMob integration, disabled in this testing build. If enabled in a future build, Google may process device identifiers and advertising data under its privacy policy. Consent and privacy controls must be configured before that release. This text must be reviewed against the actual production configuration before store submission.

Open-source projects

  • Three.js — 3D rendering, MIT.
  • cannon-es — physics collisions, MIT.
  • nipplejs — touch joystick, MIT.
  • Capacitor — Android packaging, MIT.
  • Capacitor Community AdMob — optional advertising bridge, MIT.

All car shapes, scenery and synthesized engine sounds were created for this project. No commercial car brands, music tracks or paid assets are included. Full upstream notices are distributed with the source and bundled app.
